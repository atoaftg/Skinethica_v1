Meet the doctor

Skin expertise,
with a human touch.

Dr. Poornima Chaubey is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Dermatology, Venereology & Leprosy at Hind Institute of Medical Sciences, Lucknow — bringing over 8 years of clinical and academic experience in dermatology.

With a Fellowship in Aesthetic Medicine from the Aesthetics & Skin Institute (Australia) and the Academy of Dermatologists Mumbai, her approach is rooted in evidence-based care. Her philosophy: every patient deserves honest advice, tailored treatment, and results that feel authentically themselves — never overdone, always ethical.
